The Church Explored

Structure, Worship, Mission, and Life Together

The church is not merely a building, event, platform, or loose network of isolated believers. Christ gathers His people into visible covenant life together: formed through worship, ordered under His Word, and sent into the world as His witnesses.

Christ Gathers. The Word Forms. The Church Witnesses.

This cluster explores the church as Christ’s gathered people living together under His reign until He returns.

Cluster Overview

The Visible People of God

This Resource Cluster explores the nature of the church, the gathered life of worship, fellowship and covenant community, leadership and shared ministry, mission and witness, and the church as an embassy of Christ’s Kingdom in the world.

Gathered

Christ Forms a People

The church is not isolated spirituality. Christ gathers believers into one body, household, temple, and covenant people under His reign.

Ordered

The Word Shapes the Church

The church is formed through worship, Scripture, prayer, ordinances, shepherding leadership, discipline, restoration, and shared ministry.

Sent

The Church Bears Witness

The gathered church is sent into the world as a visible witness to Christ’s Kingdom through truth, mercy, mission, holiness, and love.
