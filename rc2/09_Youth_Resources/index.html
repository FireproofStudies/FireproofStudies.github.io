Fireproof Youth Resources

A 12-session youth discipleship track on the church as Christ’s gathered people.

These resources adapt The Church Explored for teenagers and youth groups, helping students think deeply, participate faithfully, and understand why church life matters.

Youth • Church • Discipleship • Community

Built for Volunteers. Written for Teens.

Each session pairs a leader guide with a student handout for a clear, ready-to-use youth ministry experience.

How This Works

Leader Guide + Student Handout

Each youth session is designed as a complete teaching unit. The leader guide helps the volunteer lead the room; the student handout reinforces the core ideas for students.

For Leaders

Youth Leader Guides

Front-and-back printable guides with the big idea, key Scripture, opening activity, teaching flow, discussion questions, leader tips, weekly challenge, and prayer focus.

For Students

Youth Handouts

One-page student-facing handouts with the core lesson, memory verse, reflection questions, weekly challenge, and prayer focus.

12-Session Track

The Church Explored for Youth

This quarter-length track leaves room for special sessions, worship nights, service projects, holidays, or testimony nights.
