The Course Movement

From Lostness to Rooted Life

The course never assumes that every student is converted. It explains the Gospel carefully, invites honest response, and follows new life into assurance and faithful practice.

Sessions 1-4

Lostness and Human Need

Lost belonging, self-rule, helplessness, and the two humanities.

Sessions 5-7

Christ and His Work

The eternal Son made human, the cross, and bodily resurrection.

Sessions 8-11

Response and New Life

The Spirit's witness, repentance, faith, and life-giving union with Christ.

Sessions 12-13

Assurance and Practice

Confidence in Christ and the new life taking root and becoming visible.

Pastoral Guardrail

Fruit Never Becomes the Price

The course does not force professions or assume conversion. It presents repentance and faith honestly, keeps new birth as the Spirit's life-giving work in union with Christ, and treats Scripture, prayer, church, obedience, service, and witness as the appointed life and fruit of believers—not conditions for creating or preserving salvation.
