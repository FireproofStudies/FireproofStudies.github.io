One Coherent Gospel Journey

Thirteen Sessions. One Saving Work.

Found never treats salvation as a collection of detached benefits. The course keeps Jesus Christ Himself at the center from the first question to the final call to rooted life.

Lostness and Human Need

Sessions 1-4: belonging, self-rule, helplessness, and the two humanities.

Christ and His Work

Sessions 5-7: the eternal Son made human, the cross, and bodily resurrection.

Response and New Life

Sessions 8-11: the Spirit’s witness, repentance, faith, and union with Christ.

Assurance and Practice

Sessions 12-13: confidence in Christ and the new life becoming visible.
