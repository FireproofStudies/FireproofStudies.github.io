New from Resource Cluster 3

What does “saved” mean in this passage?

The New Testament does not use the word saved to mean one thing. It means a woman’s bleeding stopped. It means a ship’s company reached shore. It means a sinner passed from death into life in Christ.

What Does It Mean to Be Saved? examines every New Testament use of saving language in its own context — the peril overcome, the deliverance given, the agent, the means, and the result. 122 occurrences examined, grouped into 91 completed passage studies.
