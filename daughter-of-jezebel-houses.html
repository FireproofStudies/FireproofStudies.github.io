The Biblical Tension

Dynastic Power Meets Covenant Promise

Omri’s house brings military strength, international alliances, and royal centralization. David’s house rests on a promise that cannot be reduced to palace control. The novel’s central conflict grows from Athaliah’s conviction that the first can master the second.

The title follows the novel’s traditional identification of Athaliah as Jezebel’s daughter. Scripture calls her both a daughter of Ahab’s house and a “daughter of Omri,” language that may function dynastically as well as genealogically.
