Suggested Reading Flow

Building the Framework

These articles are interconnected. The recommended order below progressively develops the cluster’s central argument.

1. Did the Church Begin in Matthew, John, or Acts?

Establishes the foundational framework for understanding the formation and launch of the church.

2. John 20 and Pentecost

Clarifies the distinction between resurrection preparation and Pentecostal empowerment.

3. Peter, Pentecost, and the Keys

Demonstrates the covenantal expansion of the church through Peter’s ministry.

4. What Happened at Pentecost?

Provides the broad theological picture of Pentecost and mission.

5. The Church at Pentecost Was Not Yet Mature

Explains progressive apostolic development after Pentecost.

6. Why the Church Had to Wait

Connects Pentecost to Christ’s exaltation and heavenly enthronement.
