Resource Cluster One

RC1 Leader Sheets

Teaching guides for The Launch of the Church: Wind in the Sails. These sheets are designed to help pastors, teachers, and group leaders present the workbook material clearly, sequentially, and faithfully.

How to Use These Sheets

  • Read the assigned workbook section first.
  • Use the leader sheet as a presentation roadmap.
  • Open the PNG for quick viewing or sharing.
  • Use the PDF for printing or classroom distribution.
  • Pair each lesson with the recommended visuals and glossary terms.
Teaching principle: the workbook provides the doctrinal foundation; the leader sheets guide presentation, discussion, application, and prayer.
