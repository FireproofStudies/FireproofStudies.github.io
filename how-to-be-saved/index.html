Gospel Foundations · Booklet 1

How to Be Saved

A Clear Invitation to Life in Christ

The gospel is God’s good news concerning His Son: Christ died for our sins, was buried, rose again, and saves all who trust in Him.

Read OnlineDownload Print Booklet
Why We Need Him

You Were Made for Life with God

God made you. You are not an accident, nor do you belong finally to yourself. He made human beings in His image and gave us a place in His world under His good rule.

Your life is His gift. But He made you for more than survival, achievement, or momentary happiness. True life is fellowship with God: knowing Him, receiving His love, living under His wise authority, and becoming what He created you to be.

“And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.”
John 17:3

We Have Turned from God

The Bible calls our turning from God sin. Sin includes the things we do that God forbids, but it goes deeper. Sin is self-rule: the claim that we have the right to decide what is good, direct our own lives, and use God’s gifts without yielding to God Himself.

We must see this not only in the world around us, but in ourselves. We have all chosen our own way. Sin separates us from the God who is the source of life, leaves us guilty before Him, and moves us toward death. Good deeds cannot erase guilt already incurred. We need more than advice. We need rescue.

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.”
Romans 3:23
What God Has Done

God Has Acted in Jesus Christ

The good news is that God has not left us in our rebellion. Jesus Christ is God’s eternal Son, who became man. He entered our world of weakness, sorrow, temptation, suffering, and death, yet He did not repeat our rebellion. He lived in perfect obedience to the Father.

Then He died for our sins. At the cross, the innocent Son gave Himself for guilty sinners. He bore what we could not bear and opened the way of forgiveness and peace with God.

Jesus did not remain in the grave. He rose again the third day. His resurrection declares that death has not conquered Him. He lives, and He is able to give life to those who come to Him.

“Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures.”
1 Corinthians 15:3–4
Your Response

Come to Christ: Turn and Trust

Because God has acted in Christ, He now calls you to respond. Repentance and faith are not two religious tasks to complete. They are one turn. Repentance means turning from sin and self-rule. Faith means turning to Jesus Christ in trusting surrender.

Saving faith is more than agreeing that the gospel is true. A person may understand the message, feel its force, or admire Jesus while still holding his own life back from Christ. Saving faith says, “I give myself to Jesus Christ. I will no longer trust myself to save or rule me. I place my guilt, my future, and my whole life in His hands.”

This is not a work by which you earn salvation. Salvation is God’s gift. Yet He calls you to receive His Son truly—not merely His benefits, but Christ Himself.

“Repent ye, and believe the gospel.”
Mark 1:15
The Gift of New Life

What God Gives When You Trust Christ

When you turn from self-rule and entrust yourself to Christ, God does more than change your religious identity. The Holy Spirit joins you to the risen Son and gives you new life.

The sinner believes; the Spirit gives life.

Jesus called this being born again. Because you belong to Christ, God forgives your sins, brings you into peace with Himself, receives you into His family, gives you His Spirit, and gives you a future that death cannot destroy. These blessings belong together because they are found in Christ.

Do Not Put Off the Christ Who Calls You

Do not confuse understanding with surrender, emotion with faith, or repeating words with coming to Jesus Himself.

You do not need to find the right words or repeat a formula. You need to come honestly to Jesus Christ—turning from self-rule and entrusting yourself to Him.

Speak to God plainly. Ask Him for mercy. Tell Him that you are placing your life in the hands of His Son. But do not trust the words you say; trust Christ Himself.

If someone gave you this booklet, tell that person today: “I have trusted Christ,” or “I want to come to Christ and need help taking that step.”

“Him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.”
John 6:37
