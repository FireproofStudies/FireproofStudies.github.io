Where’s the Fire?

By James Burke • 1 min read
Back to Articles

The Vision Behind Fireproof Commentaries

In 1 Corinthians 3:12-15, the Apostle Paul speaks of a coming fire. This isn't the fire of judgment for the lost, but a fire of testing for the work of the believer. He describes two kinds of builders: those who use gold, silver, and precious stones, and those who use wood, hay, and stubble.

The name Fireproof Commentaries was born from a desire to help the local church build with the right materials. Too often, modern ministry is built on "stubble"—fleeting emotions, cultural trends, and shallow programs. These may look impressive today, but they will not stand the test of the fire.

"Every man's work shall be made manifest: for the day shall declare it, because it shall be revealed by fire; and the fire shall try every man's work of what sort it is." — 1 Corinthians 3:13

Our goal is to provide tools that help pastors, teachers, and students stay rooted in the "gold" of God's Word. Whether it is through our main commentary series, our teen curriculum, or children's books, we are committed to building things that last.
