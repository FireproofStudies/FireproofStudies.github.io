What Is Where to Stand?

Where to Stand with James Burke is a short-form teaching series connected to the themes of the Where To trilogy. The episodes address the instability many people feel when truth, identity, effort, and meaning no longer seem able to hold them up.

These videos are designed to be thoughtful, accessible, and direct—brief enough to watch easily, but substantial enough to help you think more clearly about the Christian life.

Core Themes

What the Series Explores

Truth

How can a person stand in a world where certainty is treated as arrogance and confusion is treated as wisdom?

Identity

Why does knowing who you are still fail if your identity is not grounded in something outside yourself?

Effort

Why do balance, discipline, and doing everything right still collapse when they are forced to carry what only Christ can bear?

Stability

What does it mean to stand, walk, and rest in Christ when the systems beneath your life begin to break?
