Commentary

Colony of Heaven

A Christ-centered exposition of Ephesians exploring the church as a heavenly people living faithfully in the world under the reign of Christ.

In Colony of Heaven, Pastor James Burke explores the profound reality of the church’s identity in Christ. Ephesians reveals a people blessed in heavenly places, united in one body, and called to walk worthy of their calling while living in the present world.

This volume helps readers understand the riches of God’s grace, the unity of the church, the new life of believers, the order of Christian households, and the necessity of standing firm in the whole armor of God.

Ephesians Commentary

A Heavenly People in an Earthly World

A pastoral exposition designed to help churches understand identity, unity, calling, and spiritual strength in Christ.

What This Book Covers

Themes in Ephesians

This volume traces the movement of Ephesians from heavenly blessing to earthly faithfulness.

In Christ

Ephesians begins with the believer’s standing in Christ, where every spiritual blessing is secured by the grace and purpose of God.

One New Man

Jew and Gentile are reconciled in Christ and brought together into one body, revealing the wisdom and grace of God.

The Church

The church is presented as Christ’s body, God’s household, and a living temple being built together for the dwelling of God through the Spirit.

Worthy Walking

Doctrine becomes visible in conduct as believers are called to walk in unity, holiness, love, light, wisdom, and submission to Christ.

Household Order

Ephesians applies the lordship of Christ to marriage, family, work, authority, and ordinary relationships.

Spiritual Warfare

The church stands not in its own strength, but in the armor of God and the victory of Christ.

Exposition Preview

From the Exposition

“The church is not merely a social club or a weekly gathering; it is an outpost of eternity.

In Ephesians, we see that God has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places. To live as a ‘Colony of Heaven’ means bringing the values, the peace, and the authority of our true King into our everyday lives here on earth.”
