This index accounts for every occurrence in the Phase One corpus: every use of σῴζω and διασῴζω in the principal Greek texts, together with every KJV use of save, saved, savest, saveth, or saving that denotes rescue, preservation, healing, deliverance, or the securing of life.

Every reference in blue links to its completed study. A dagger (†) marks an occurrence affected by a textual difference between the principal Greek editions.

How this list was built

The inventory is bidirectional: every occurrence of the Greek saving verbs traced to each English rendering, and every KJV saving expression traced back to the Greek behind it. Greek data is drawn from the Tyndale House edition and Hodges–Farstad's Majority Text, with Scrivener's Textus Receptus consulted where it explains a KJV reading. Displayed Scripture is the King James Version.

Direct verification of every occurrence against the printed Hodges–Farstad second edition is in progress and is recorded separately.
