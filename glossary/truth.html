Biblical & Theological Glossary

Truth

Truth is reality as it is, revealed by the God who cannot lie.

Because God is true, everything He reveals is true. He makes truth knowable through His creation, His providence, the human conscience, and supremely through His written Word and His Son, Jesus Christ. All truth is God's truth and therefore cannot ultimately contradict itself. While Scripture alone is God's inspired, infallible revelation and the final authority for faith and practice, every truth rightly discovered bears witness to the wisdom and faithfulness of its Creator. Truth is therefore to be believed, obeyed, and proclaimed.

See in Scripture: Psalm 19:1–7; Psalm 119:160; John 14:6; John 17:17; Romans 1:18–20; Romans 3:4; Titus 1:2

See also: Revelation, Scripture, Doctrine, Faith, Gospel
