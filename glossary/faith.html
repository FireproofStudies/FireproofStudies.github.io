Biblical & Theological Glossary

Faith

Faith is trusting reliance upon God as He has revealed Himself in Jesus Christ.

Biblical faith is not mere belief or emotion, but trust that brings life into alignment with truth.

See in Scripture: Hebrews 11:1; Romans 1:17

See also: Assurance, Salvation, Truth
