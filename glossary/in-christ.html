Biblical & Theological Glossary

In Christ

To be in Christ is to stand within the reality of the One in whom God's saving purpose is fulfilled.

It is participation in His completion, not striving for our own.

See in Scripture: Ephesians 1:3–10; Romans 8:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17

See also: Election, Predestination, Adoption
Related Study: The Renewed Mind • Teaching 1 Corinthians Without Division • Fellowship Beyond Friendship • Baptism, the Lord’s Supper, and Embodied Christianity
