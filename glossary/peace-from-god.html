Peace from God

Peace from God is the settled rest God gives His children as they live within the right relationship and purpose He restores through Christ. Its strength may vary with faith and circumstance.

Peace from God is a real gift in prayer, suffering, fear, and daily life. It is not the test of whether a person has peace with God. Feelings may change; the believer’s standing in Christ does not.

God’s peace steadies His children as they learn to trust Him and live within the order and purpose He gives them in Christ.

See in Scripture: John 14:25–27; Philippians 4:4–9; Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Peter 5:6–11
