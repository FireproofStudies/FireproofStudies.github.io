Biblical & Theological Glossary

Freedom

Freedom is the life God gives those in Christ to live as they were made to live — not independence from God, but release from sin, self-rule, condemnation, and death so they may willingly love and obey Him.

The world calls autonomy freedom, but self-rule is bondage: the servant of sin cannot free himself. True freedom is being loosed from that slavery to serve God gladly — if the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed.

It is not license to sin but liberty from it; not the absence of a master, but the exchange of a cruel one for the rightful and good One.

See in Scripture: John 8:31–36; Romans 6:17–22; Romans 8:1–2; Galatians 5:1, 13
