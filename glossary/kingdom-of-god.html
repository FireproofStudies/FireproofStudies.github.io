Biblical & Theological Glossary

Kingdom of God

The Kingdom of God is the rightful reign of God established in Jesus Christ, present now wherever He is trusted and obeyed, and awaiting full visible completion at His return.

It is not a geographical territory, but the active rule of God over redeemed hearts and history.

See in Scripture: Mark 1:14–15; Luke 17:20–21; Romans 14:17

See also: Gospel, Church, Worship
Related Study: Peter, Pentecost, and the Keys of the Kingdom • The Church as Embassy of the Kingdom • Why the Church Had to Wait • What Is the Church?
In the Saved gateway: John 3:1–21 • Mark 10:17–31 • Luke 13:22–30 • Luke 18:18–30 • Matthew 19:16–30 • 2 Timothy 4:16–18 • Luke 23:26–43
