Biblical & Theological Glossary

Glory

Glory is the revealed worth, beauty, and visible display of who God truly is, most fully revealed in Jesus Christ.

The glory of God is most clearly revealed in Christ, where holiness, truth, justice, mercy, love, and power are displayed together.

See in Scripture: Psalm 19:1; Isaiah 6:1–3; John 1:14; John 17:1–5

See also: Love, Sovereignty, Worship
Related Study: The Renewed Mind • Worship Before Mission • Household, Temple, and Holy Nation • The Church Is Not a Product
In the Saved gateway: Hebrews 5:5–10 • Revelation 21:22–27 • John 11:1–16 • Romans 8:18–30 • Mark 8:34–9:1 • Luke 9:23–27 • John 12:20–36
